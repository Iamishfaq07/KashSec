Frequently asked questions

I have zero experience. Can I really do this?

Yes — the course is built exactly for you. Start at path 1 and go in order. Every term is explained in plain words with everyday examples, and nothing is assumed.

Is it really free?

Completely. Every lesson stays free forever. No card, no paywall, no upsell.

Do I need to buy anything or have a powerful computer?

No. You'll install free software (VirtualBox + Kali Linux) that runs on a normal laptop. If your machine is very old, free browser labs like TryHackMe still let you practise.

Is any of this illegal?

Not the way we teach it. You only ever practise on your own lab, on free legal training sites, or in-scope bug bounty programs. Path 01 covers the legal golden rule in detail.

How should I go through the course?

Top to bottom, in order. Each path builds on the one before. Do the labs and challenges — hands-on practice is what makes it stick.

How long does the whole course take?

At a sensible pace (a sub-lesson a day), the full course takes 3–4 months. Depth beats speed.
