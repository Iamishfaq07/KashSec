Governance, Risk, and Compliance — the non-coding path into security. ISO 27001, NIST CSF, SOC 2, vendor risk, and how to actually break into a GRC career.

  12. Other Compliance Frameworks: PCI-DSS, HIPAA & Beyond — Why one framework rarely covers an entire organisation.
