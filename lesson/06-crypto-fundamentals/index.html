Path 06: Cryptography Fundamentals — KashSec Academy

Crypto concepts get used everywhere in this course — this path teaches them from zero: encoding vs encryption vs hashing, AES, RSA, certificates, and the mistakes that keep breaking real systems.

Sub-lessons

  • 06.1 · What is cryptography? — Encoding vs. encryption vs. hashing — the distinction that unlocks everything else.
  • 06.2 · Symmetric Encryption — One shared key, AES, and why ECB mode is a classic mistake.
  • 06.3 · Asymmetric Encryption — Public/private keys, RSA, hybrid encryption, and digital signatures.
  • 06.4 · Key Exchange & Diffie-Hellman — How two strangers agree on a shared secret over a channel an attacker is watching.
  • 06.5 · Elliptic Curve Cryptography — The other hard math problem behind modern crypto, and why smaller keys win.
  • 06.6 · Hashing Explained — SHA-256, why passwords need bcrypt not SHA-256, and salting.
  • 06.7 · Digital Signatures & Certificates — How the padlock icon actually works, and what it doesn't promise.
  • 06.8 · The TLS Handshake, Step by Step — How every earlier lesson in this path assembles into the padlock icon.
  • 06.9 · Common Cryptographic Attacks — Padding oracle, replay, downgrade, and side-channel attacks, named and explained.
  • 06.10 · Common Crypto Mistakes — Seven recurring real-world failure patterns, gathered in one place.
  • 06.11 · Applied Cryptography: Choosing and Using It Correctly — Libraries over primitives, key management, and a practical decision guide.
  • 06.12 · Blockchain & Cryptocurrency Cryptography — Hash chains, wallets, and signatures — this path's concepts in a real-world system.
  • 06.13 · Post-Quantum Cryptography — Shor's algorithm, harvest-now-decrypt-later, and why NIST already standardized the fix.
  • 06.14 · Crypto Hands-On Lab — CyberChef and OpenSSL — encode, encrypt, hash, and inspect real certificates.
