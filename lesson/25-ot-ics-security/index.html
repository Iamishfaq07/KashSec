KashSec Academy · Path 25 — OT/ICS & Critical Infrastructure Security

Path 25: OT/ICS & Critical Infrastructure Security — KashSec Academy

A different world from IT: SCADA, industrial protocols, the Purdue Model, real-world ICS attacks (Stuxnet, Ukraine grid), and practical controls that respect physical safety constraints.

Sub-lessons

  • 25.1 · What is OT/ICS Security? — Why OT security reverses IT's usual priorities, and the vocabulary you'll need.
  • 25.2 · SCADA & ICS Architecture Fundamentals — The sense-decide-act control loop, SCADA vs. DCS, HMI, and the historian.
  • 25.3 · Industrial Protocols: Modbus, DNP3, and Why Their Design Causes Security Problems — Protocols with no built-in authentication, and why 'just add TLS' doesn't work.
  • 25.4 · The Purdue Model: Segmenting IT from OT — The standard reference architecture, and why the DMZ is the whole point.
  • 25.5 · Real-World ICS Attacks: What They Actually Teach Us — Stuxnet, the Ukraine grid attacks, Oldsmar, and Triton — mapped back to the lessons that predicted them.
  • 25.6 · Practical OT Security Controls — Segmentation, passive monitoring, jump hosts, and compensating controls for unpatchable legacy gear.
  • 25.7 · OT Asset Inventory & Visibility — Passive discovery for the equipment you don't even know you have.
  • 25.8 · ICS Vulnerability Management — Exposure-based prioritization when active scanning itself is the risk.
  • 25.9 · Safety Instrumented Systems — The independent last line of defense against physical harm — and the exact target of Triton/Trisis.
  • 25.10 · ICS Incident Response — Why 'isolate immediately' can be more dangerous than the incident itself.
  • 25.11 · ICS Supply Chain Risk — Compromised firmware, infected removable media, and third-party integrator access.
  • 25.12 · IIoT & Cloud Convergence — How a single cellular sensor can quietly bypass the entire Purdue Model.
  • 25.13 · OT/ICS Careers & Certifications — GICSP, realistic entry paths, and how to build demonstrable experience without plant access.
  • 25.14 · Capstone: Secure a Realistic Industrial Network — A full assessment exercise applying everything in this path to one fictional facility.
