How to use it

  1. Load the sample capture.
  2. Identify the protocols present.
  3. Follow a conversation between two addresses and read what crossed it.

What to take away

Plaintext protocols leak everything

HTTP, FTP and Telnet carry credentials in the clear. Finding them in a capture is often the fastest win in an investigation.

Metadata survives encryption

TLS hides content, not who talked to whom, when, how often, or how much. Beaconing malware is frequently caught on timing alone.

Frequently asked questions

What is a pcap file?

A recorded capture of network packets, written by tools like tcpdump or Wireshark, which can be replayed and analysed after the fact.

Can I see passwords in captured traffic?

In plaintext protocols, yes — which is precisely why they were replaced. In TLS traffic you see metadata only, unless you hold the keys.
