Vendor Risk Tiering Simulator

Six vendors, six very different data-access levels. Tier them correctly and decide who needs a full security review.

Six vendors with very different levels of access to your data. Tier them and decide who needs a full security review — because reviewing everyone equally means reviewing everyone badly.

How to use it

  1. Review what data each vendor touches.
  2. Assign a tier based on access and criticality.
  3. Decide the depth of review each tier warrants.

What to take away

Tier by data access and criticality

A vendor holding customer records is a different problem from one supplying office snacks. Spend the review budget accordingly.

Fourth parties count

Your vendor's vendors touch your data too. Concentration risk hides one layer down, where everyone turns out to depend on the same provider.

Onboarding is not the end

Access grows, products change, companies get acquired. Tiering needs periodic review or it describes a company that no longer exists.

Frequently asked questions

What decides a vendor's tier?

Sensitivity of data accessed, whether they connect to your systems, how badly their outage hurts you, and any regulatory exposure their failure creates.

Is a SOC 2 report enough assurance?

It is evidence, not a verdict. Read the scope, the period covered, and especially the exceptions — a clean report on a narrow scope can say very little about what you care about.
