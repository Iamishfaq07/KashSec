Clickjacking Simulator

An invisible iframe sits on top of a fake game button. See exactly how a real click lands on a hidden "delete account" button instead.

An invisible frame sits over a fake game button. You think you are clicking Play; the click lands on "delete account" in a framed page where you are still logged in. Seeing it once makes the defence obvious.

How to use it

  1. Click the visible button.
  2. Reveal the transparent frame above it.
  3. See where the click actually landed.

What to take away

Nothing is forged

The victim really clicked, really authenticated, really authorised it. Only the intent was stolen — which is why server-side checks cannot detect it.

Framing controls are the fix

X-Frame-Options: DENY, or the modern CSP frame-ancestors directive, tells the browser not to allow framing at all.

Frequently asked questions

How do I stop clickjacking?

Send Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'none' (or an explicit allowlist). X-Frame-Options is the older equivalent and still worth sending for legacy clients.

What is likejacking?

The same technique aimed at social media buttons, harvesting likes, follows and shares from users who thought they clicked something else.
