# Practical Reconnaissance Lab

Hands-on OSINT and reconnaissance practice.

## Tools to use
- Google dorks
- Shodan
- theHarvester
- Maltego Community Edition (free)

## Lab tasks
1. Find public information about a company (use a fictional or your own test company)
2. Discover subdomains of a target
3. Look for leaked credentials on Have I Been Pwned (ethically)

## Challenge
Document your findings in a simple report format (what you searched, what you found, why it matters).
