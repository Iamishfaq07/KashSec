# Advanced Wireshark Lab

Go deeper with packet analysis.

## Lab tasks
1. Capture traffic during a login and filter for credentials or cookies
2. Follow TCP streams to reconstruct a full conversation
3. Use display filters effectively (http, dns, tcp.flags)
4. Export objects (images, files) from a capture
5. Analyze a provided packet capture for anomalies or attacks

## Challenge
Identify at least 3 interesting things in a capture file and explain what they mean.
