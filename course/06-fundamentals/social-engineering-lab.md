# Social Engineering Lab

Understand how attackers manipulate people.

## Safe practice exercises
1. Analyze 5 real phishing emails (use public examples or simulated ones)
2. Identify red flags in each
3. Role-play a vishing (voice phishing) scenario with a friend
4. Create a fake login page warning poster for awareness

## Key lessons
- People are the weakest link
- Always verify before acting
- Small details give away scams

## Challenge
Write a 1-page awareness guide for your family or friends about common scams.
