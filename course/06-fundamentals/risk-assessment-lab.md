# Risk Assessment Lab

Practice thinking like a risk analyst.

## Exercise
1. Pick a small company or personal setup (home network, small business website)
2. Identify assets (data, systems, people)
3. List threats and vulnerabilities
4. Estimate likelihood and impact
5. Suggest simple mitigations

## Challenge
Create a one-page risk register for your chosen scenario.
