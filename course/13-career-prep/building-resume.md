# Building your security resume

Your resume is your first impression.

## Key sections for security roles
- Contact & links (LinkedIn, GitHub, personal site)
- Summary / Objective (tailored to the job)
- Skills (tools, languages, frameworks)
- Projects & labs (Kali setups, bug bounty reports, CTFs)
- Certifications
- Experience (even internships or self-projects count)

## Tips
- Quantify everything ("Reduced false positives by 40%")
- Use keywords from the job description
- Keep it to 1 page if possible
