# Security Mindset Lab

Develop the right way of thinking about security.

## Exercises
1. List 10 everyday objects and think of 3 ways each could be attacked or misused
2. Analyze a recent news story about a breach — what failed?
3. For your own devices, list the biggest risks and simple fixes

## Challenge
Write a short "security mindset" checklist you can use when using or building anything digital.
