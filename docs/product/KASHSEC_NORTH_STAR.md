# North star

KashSec is a free-first place to learn a security idea, try it on a fictional target, and show what you did. Accounts stay optional.

## Starter journey shipped in this change

1. Open a skill in `/skills/` and read why it is next.
2. Open the linked lesson, lab, or tool that already existed.
3. Repair the fictional invoice policy in `/fix-verify/` and read the original case in `/research/kashsec-invoice-boundary`.
4. Put that skill on a Security+, OSCP+, or SC-200 plan in `/certlab/`.

The career roadmap at `#/roadmap` remains the career destination. The skills graph links to role names; it does not create a second roadmap.

## Not shipped

CyberCity, classrooms, a public writeup uploader, Skill Passport profiles, and an AI coach. Those stay later, and community uploads stay off until moderation exists.
