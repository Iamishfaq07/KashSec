Portfolio Project Generator

Guided starter briefs for real portfolio projects, with a write-up template you can export.

The gap between "I did an online course" and "I can do this job" is closed by projects you can show and talk about. This page generates a project brief with scope and deliverables, so you have something concrete to build rather than another tutorial to watch.

How to use it

  1. Generate a project brief.
  2. Read the scope and deliverables.
  3. Download the brief as Markdown and use it as your README.

What to take away

Write it up, or it did not happen

The write-up is the portfolio piece. What you tried, what failed, what you concluded — that is what shows judgement, and judgement is what gets hired.

Small and finished beats ambitious and abandoned

One tidy completed project you can explain in detail is worth more in an interview than three half-built repositories.

Stay legal

Build against your own lab, deliberately vulnerable applications, or systems you have written permission to test. Never a live third party.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a good security portfolio project?

One that shows a decision, not just a tool run. A home lab with a documented detection you wrote and tested beats a screenshot of a scanner's output every time.

Do I need a GitHub account?

It helps, because it gives you somewhere to link from your CV, and public commit history is evidence in itself. A well-written blog post works too.

How long should a project take?

A weekend to two weeks is the useful range. Long enough to hit real problems, short enough that you actually finish and write it up.
